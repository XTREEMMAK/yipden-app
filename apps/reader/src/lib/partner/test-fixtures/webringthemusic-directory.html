Dave Hawkins
bandcamp 		links.iamdavehawkins.com Dave Hawkins writes songs meant for slowing down in a fast-moving world. Based in Denver, operating out of a home studio, he blends his years of experience playing classical piano with synth textures and indie guitar rock to create something reflective of where we're at. At the end of the day - it's honest music about just how strange it feels to be alive right now.

Brandon operates as a solo artist, writing, producing & releasing music from his basement in central Massachusetts.
